Aangan Call Flow

How an enquiry call becomes a booked consultation, laid out in the six stages: trigger, input, context, processing, AI and output.

  • During the callEverything the agent knows comes from the Vaani dashboard, including today's date, which is refreshed every night.
  • After the callEvery completed call shows up on the dashboard with its recording and transcript. Only qualified leads go to Telegram and HubSpot.
  • Designer loopA designer claims the lead and picks one of the caller's windows. Cal.com books it, and the HubSpot deal moves to "Consultation booked".
